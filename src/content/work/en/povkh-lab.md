## Context

POVKH LAB is an independent music label founded in Brescia, Italy. This is a founder-led project, not an external client engagement. The case records how a label identity, a multilingual website, and the connected release workflow were shaped into one working system.

## Constraint

The work needed to make a catalogue feel deliberate without pretending that a pre-launch system was a commercial success story. Music, artwork, typography, editorial copy, audio playback, and listening links had to read as parts of one label rather than as disconnected screens. Public launch gates also had to remain visible while contacts and other operational inputs were still being confirmed.

## Role and strategy

I owned the strategy, identity direction, information architecture, interface design, implementation, content structure, and QA. The strategy was to treat every release touchpoint as a single route through the label: discover the catalogue, understand a release, play or choose a service, and return to the archive without losing context.

## Identity

The visual system uses a dark technical field, Signal Red accents, a condensed display voice, and monospaced metadata. The identity rules are documented in the source brand guide; the case uses only production masters and verified site captures. Concept explorations remain outside the published case and are not presented as launched work.

## Information architecture

The site separates label context, catalogue, release details, artist context, listening choices, process, press, contact, and recovery. A release page can lead to a label-owned listening chooser, so the smart-link behavior stays understandable even when an external platform is unavailable. The same route logic is mirrored for English, Italian, and Russian source content; this case publishes an English and Russian reading with matching evidence and disclosure.

## Audio, catalogue, and smart-link behavior

The catalogue source distinguishes verified public destinations from upcoming or incomplete inputs. The local player uses a precomputed waveform, supports explicit play controls, and keeps a plain link path available when JavaScript is absent. A listening chooser presents verified platform destinations without a redirect service or third-party aggregator dependency. These are implementation behaviors documented in the source site handoff, not claims about audience growth.

## QA and result boundary

The source project records keyboard, responsive, reduced-motion, accessibility, no-tracker, and no-external-CDN checks. This case publishes the evidence as a transparent pre-launch system: an identity and digital workflow with documented constraints. It does not claim client revenue, conversion lift, audience growth, launch approval, or any other unsupported commercial result.
