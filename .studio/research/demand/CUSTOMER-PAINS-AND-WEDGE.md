# Customer pains and initial market wedge

Status: research recommendation for human review; not approved positioning or final public copy.
Observed: 2026-08-21.
Scope: Russian B2B digital-service market relevant to Povkh.Dev.

## Verdict

Povkh.Dev should not enter the market as another universal agency for websites, branding, CRM, SEO and advertising. That surface is crowded, each category contains materially different products, and the current proof does not support a claim of superior price, speed or commercial performance across all of them.

The strongest initial wedge is narrower:

> Established owner-led and specialist B2B businesses that already have a real product or service, but cannot turn it into one clear, trustworthy and measurable path from explanation to inquiry.

The bounded job is to diagnose and then connect the commercial meaning, brand presentation, website and inquiry path. CRM, analytics, SEO and advertising are downstream modules only when their scope, owner and evidence are separately confirmed.

The practical entry product is a paid or explicitly bounded commercial-route diagnosis. Its result is a fact-based recommendation: what is broken, what must be clarified, what should be built first, what can remain, what evidence is required and how the future action can be measured. It is not a free generic consultation and it does not guarantee leads.

## Evidence boundary

The sources support buyer-selection criteria, market density, common service packaging and the technical need to connect promotion with target actions and measurement. They do not constitute interviews with Povkh.Dev prospects. Therefore the pains below are marked as `observed`, `inferred` or `unknown`.

## The piece to take

### Primary customer

An owner, commercial lead or marketing lead in an established Russian B2B or specialist B2B2C company who is launching, repositioning or rebuilding a product/direction and sees at least two connected problems:

- the offer is hard to understand or compare;
- the brand and materials look fragmented;
- the website does not support the sales conversation;
- the desired inquiry is unclear or not measured;
- several contractors would otherwise need to be coordinated.

No exact company-size, industry or budget threshold is approved yet. “Large business” remains a future segment, not the first entry point.

### Job Povkh.Dev can credibly own

Turn a complex commercial offer into a coherent buyer route and, after approval, implement the agreed brand/site layer with an explicit inquiry and measurement design.

This is intentionally narrower than “we build the whole marketing system”. Povkh.Dev owns the clarity and connection of the customer-facing route. It only owns CRM delivery, acquisition and ongoing optimisation when those modules have confirmed scope, capability and proof.

### Why this wedge fits

1. The market is highly competitive; broad labels provide little differentiation. Current Workspace data shows thousands of providers and a buying process increasingly shaped by relevant cases and specialisation.
2. Existing Povkh.Dev evidence is strongest where a specialised subject had to be explained and turned into a structured digital path: ENDOkey and the founder-led POVKH LAB system.
3. The current process already supports an evidence-first diagnosis, explicit scope, staged decisions, accessible build and quality verification.
4. The wedge avoids unsupported claims about guaranteed sales, rankings, advertising performance, lowest price or universal speed.
5. A diagnostic entry gives the buyer a useful decision before committing to a larger implementation and gives Povkh.Dev enough evidence to scope that implementation honestly.

## Offer ladder for the next Commercial Strategy packet

These are internal product roles, not final names or copy.

1. **Entry: commercial-route diagnosis.** Buying situation, offer clarity, current site/brand path, target action, evidence gaps and prioritised route.
2. **Core: meaning + brand/site system.** Approved positioning/message logic, identity application, website structure/content/design/build and verified action states.
3. **Connection module.** Forms, event plan and a documented handoff; CRM only after platform, process, access and owner are confirmed.
4. **Growth modules.** SEO requirements, content expansion, Direct or ongoing optimisation only as separately qualified scopes. They must not be implied by the core offer today.

## Selection and rejection rules

Prioritise a lead when:

- there is an existing product, service or direction to study;
- the decision maker can provide facts and participate in diagnosis;
- the problem spans meaning plus at least one customer-facing surface;
- the desired customer action can be named;
- the client accepts staged evidence and approval instead of guaranteed performance.

Do not position the offer as a fit when:

- the only criterion is the lowest possible price or an instant template;
- the client wants guaranteed leads, rankings or revenue;
- the task is only ongoing SEO, ad management or a CRM rollout without the required specialist proof;
- enterprise procurement, 24/7 support, SLA or large integration ownership is required;
- the product facts, legal claims or decision authority are unavailable.

## Proof required before public positioning

1. Convert ENDOkey into a concise problem -> decision -> artifact -> verified boundary case; add commercial metrics only if the owner supplies an approved measurement definition and evidence.
2. Keep POVKH LAB clearly founder-led and use it as implementation/system proof, not client ROI proof.
3. Add one external B2B case that demonstrates a previously fragmented offer/site/action path becoming a coherent shipped system.
4. Record actual scope, calendar duration, revision count, roles and handoff for the next projects before making speed or lean-cost claims.
5. Run 5–10 customer/prospect interviews after this provisional wedge is approved. Test the buying situation, alternatives, feared risks, budget logic and words clients use; do not ask whether they “like the positioning”.

## Decision for human review

Approve, narrow or reject this provisional choice:

> First win specialist B2B businesses with a real but hard-to-explain offer by diagnosing and then building one coherent path from commercial meaning to a measurable inquiry. Use creator-led delivery, explicit scope and evidence as trust mechanisms. Keep CRM, SEO and advertising outside the core promise until separately proven.

Approval would allow a separate Commercial Strategy packet to define value logic, offer architecture, proof sequence, risk treatment and CTA roles. It would not yet approve final page copy or site changes.
