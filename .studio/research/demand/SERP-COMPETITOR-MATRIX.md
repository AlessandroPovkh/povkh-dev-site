## Cross-cluster conclusions

- Ordinary buyer language and provider taxonomy are not interchangeable. CRM is the clearest correction: `настройка CRM` surfaces technical integrators, while `построение воронки продаж` surfaces consultants and sales-system audits.
- A low entry price often represents a narrower template, audit or setup product, not the same deliverable at lower cost.
- The strongest recurring CTA pattern is a diagnostic conversation, audit or project calculation. Direct “buy now” is uncommon for complex B2B scope.
- Published prices are useful packaging evidence but do not establish market averages, realised sales, quality or demand.
- Performance claims about leads, rankings, revenue and speed remain seller claims unless supported by independently reviewable cases and measurement definitions.
