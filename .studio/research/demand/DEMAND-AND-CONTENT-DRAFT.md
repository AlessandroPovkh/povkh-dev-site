# Demand and site-content draft

Status: preliminary, human-reviewed SERP and broad Wordstat synthesis. Regional demand is not yet collected; paid API remains unavailable under `H-008`.

## What the evidence currently supports

Povkh.Dev can credibly organise its public offer around a connected commercial path rather than an undifferentiated list of services:

`offer and brand → website → lead path and measurement → acquisition → iteration`

The current SERP shows separate demand-shaped service categories for website development, corporate branding, sales funnels, SEO and context advertising. Free web-Wordstat confirms that all five seed phrases occurred in Yandex searches during the observed period, but their broad counts are not directly comparable commercial demand: query breadth and noise differ strongly. The evidence still does not tell us which regions are easier to enter or whether users prefer a single integrated contractor.

## Recommended site narrative for the next strategy packet

1. Start from the buyer's commercial problem: unclear offer, weak site, lost inquiries or disconnected acquisition.
2. Show the route from diagnosis to an operational sales asset, not a catalogue of unrelated deliverables.
3. Give each service a concrete scope, inputs, outputs, exclusions and next CTA.
4. Separate creator-led operating-model claims from performance claims. Prove the former with process and named responsibility; use case evidence before making the latter.
5. Use one primary conversion path: discuss the commercial task → receive a scoped diagnosis/plan. Add service-specific secondary CTAs only when the funnel and CRM destination are known.

## Buyer-language correction

Research must begin with how buyers describe work, not only with agency service labels. `настройка CRM`, `ведение SEO`, `SEO оптимизация`, `настроить Директ`, `заказать сайт` and symptom phrases such as `сайт не приносит заявки` lead distinct intents. Professional terms remain useful for scoping deliverables, but should not dictate navigation or copy before buyer-language evidence is reviewed.

## Not yet allowed

- Exact-match or commercial-demand claims based on the broad counts.
- “Best region”, “low competition” or regional-entry priority.
- Final titles, descriptions, H1s or SEO copy based on unverified semantics.
- Guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, timelines or superiority.
- Claims about Povkh.Dev capabilities that are not backed by the approved project context and delivery evidence.
