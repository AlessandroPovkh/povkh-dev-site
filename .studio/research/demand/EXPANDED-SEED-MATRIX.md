## Regional comparison queue

Use the same reviewed commercial phrase set for every region. Initial comparison set: Москва, Санкт-Петербург, Екатеринбург, Казань, Новосибирск, Пермь, Челябинск, Самара, Уфа, Ростов-на-Дону, Краснодар and Нижний Новгород.

For each region record separately:

- Wordstat count/share or explicit unavailability;
- visible local-provider count in a fixed top-10 organic SERP;
- directories/aggregators versus direct providers;
- published low/mid/high offer bands;
- local proof expectations and dominant CTA;
- entry hypothesis with confidence, never a claim of “easy region”.

## Collection size

The primary buyer-language layer contains 84 seeds across six problem families. The secondary professional taxonomy contains 58 formulations. They must be collected and compared separately: buyer language informs discovery and copy; professional taxonomy informs scope and page architecture. A final semantic core will be larger than either seed list after related-query expansion, then smaller after noise removal, intent merging and deduplication.
