## Claim risks

- Do not publish “faster than any agency”, “cheapest”, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed leads or unqualified market-superiority claims.
- “Creator = executor”, fewer handoffs, transparent scope and an integrated brand → site → funnel route can be positioned as an operating model only when the actual delivery contract supports it.
- Regional priority remains unresolved until Wordstat regional facts and a consistent regional SERP comparison are available.

## Additional offer-shape observations

- Brand offers visibly split into a starter identity/mini-brandbook tier, a working system with research and positioning, and a full implementation system. Public examples range from small packages to multi-million-ruble agency work; these are seller prices, not market averages. Sources: [Kuznets](https://kuznets.agency/ceny/), [Ascend](https://www.ascendbrand.ru/prices.html), [Coruna](https://www.coruna.ru/), [Stilistica](https://stilistica.ru/services/branding/brandbook/), [Geek](https://wearegeek.ru/services/company-brand-design), [Adsgalaxy](https://adsgalaxy.ru/firmennyj-stil/), [Plenum](https://plenum.ru/services/visualsystem), [Reviva](https://reviva-agency.ru/services-identity.html), [Pixel Plus](https://pixelplus.ru/brand/), [Твой Бренд](https://www.tvoibrand.ru/uslugi-i-czenyi/razrabotka-brendbuka.html).
- Website offers separate landing, corporate, store and industrial-site scopes; at least one current public price sheet explicitly packages a factory/large-industrial-company website separately. Source: [II Site price sheet](https://ii-site.ru/docs/price.pdf).
- Context-ad offers separate setup, ongoing management and media budget. One public proposal lists setup and monthly management as different line items. Source: [HeadSEO proposal](https://headseo.ru/assets/images/files/kontekstnaya-reklama/KP-NA-CONTEXT-HeadSEO.pdf).
