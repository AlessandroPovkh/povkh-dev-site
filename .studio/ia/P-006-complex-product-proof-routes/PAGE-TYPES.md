# Page types

Status: ready for human review.

## Homepage

Purpose: establish commercial relevance, provide two compact proof paths and enable the next decision.

Required structural slots:

1. complex-product direction and bounded value;
2. buyer-situation recognition;
3. KZMS + ENDOkey social-proof pair;
4. how Povkh.Dev studies and builds;
5. offer/scope boundaries;
6. primary action initiation and direct fallback.

Owner: Povkh.Dev. Messaging owns final language after IA approval.

## Work index

Purpose: compare evidence, not merely display images.

Required card fields: subject/context, client problem, performed scope, result link and internal case link. Internal delivery statuses are excluded from public labels.

Owner: Povkh.Dev.

## Case study

Purpose: let the buyer judge method, relevance and delivered evidence.

Required structural slots:

1. status/disclosure and external evidence link;
2. subject explained before project role;
3. initial commercial/customer problem;
4. evidence/research inputs and privacy boundary;
5. key decisions and why they were made;
6. delivered surface with representative views/artifacts;
7. what the result proves and does not prove;
8. related case and scoped discussion action.

The KZMS and ENDOkey pages use the same page type so the proof is comparable. Their subject matter and claim boundaries remain distinct.
