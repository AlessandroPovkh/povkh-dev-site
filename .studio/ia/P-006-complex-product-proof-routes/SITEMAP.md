## Structural decision

The complex-product direction belongs on the homepage as the main commercial frame. KZMS and ENDOkey appear there as a paired social-proof section, not as the full case narrative. Each card links to a first-party Povkh.Dev case route; that case then links to the published external result.

The Work index remains the complete evidence directory. No new top-level `Сложные продукты` or `Кейсы` navigation item is required because the current `Работы` route already owns case discovery.

## Why the pair works

- KZMS proves industrial/product-catalogue reasoning: customer language and technical constraints became industry/task routes, catalogue filtering and an engineering-selection action.
- ENDOkey proves specialised medical-product reasoning: professional context, product explanation, trust material and live action paths are connected without Povkh.Dev claiming medical effectiveness.
- POVKH LAB remains available but is not used as one of the two primary external-client proofs.

## Constraints

- The public case describes the result as a redesign of the KZMS catalogue. Internal route names and delivery statuses do not enter public copy.
- Internal KZMS correspondence may be described only as a research input. No messages, people, clients, prices or private facts may be exposed.
- ENDOkey product and medical statements belong to the live source. The case describes Povkh.Dev work and structure; it does not adopt unverified clinical or commercial outcomes.
- Russian routes only. English parity is a later routing decision.
