# Povkh.Dev reference design critique

## Brief

Create a new visual system for Povkh.Dev while preserving the existing public copy, meaning, facts, routes, and commercial sequence. The user wants the cleanliness and execution quality of the supplied references, a strong visual impression, technical interaction features, and pink in the palette.

## Formal analysis

- Composition: all three references use a dark field, strong asymmetry, large negative areas, and a small number of high-weight focal objects. The eye lands on one headline or image before it encounters supporting detail.
- Typography: oversized neutral sans-serif headlines create scale contrast against compact utility text. Hierarchy comes from proportion and spacing rather than many styles.
- Imagery: monochrome or warm cinematic imagery is treated as a sculptural object, often cropped into circles, layers, or spatial collages rather than placed as a conventional card thumbnail.
- Color: near-black carries most of the surface. Warm image light creates atmosphere. Povkh.Dev can replace the reference orange with an electric pink signal while retaining white text and restrained secondary neutrals.
- Space and rhythm: quiet upper compositions alternate with denser proof sections. This progressive density makes long pages feel authored instead of repetitive.

## What works for Povkh.Dev

1. The restrained figure-ground relationship communicates confidence and quality: a few large elements advance while utility information recedes.
2. Large type and cinematic cases can make ENDOkey and KZMS feel like evidence with cultural weight, not small portfolio cards.
3. Technical overlays, coordinates, comparison controls, and controlled depth motion support the studio’s promise of handling complex products.

## Risk to the commercial job

The references often prioritise atmosphere over explicit decision support. If copied literally, visitors may admire the surface without understanding the offer, recognising their problem, or finding the next action.

## Constructive direction

Use the reference aesthetic as a visual shell around the already-approved sales spine:

1. Hero: one clear promise and one cinematic case object; pink marks the route to discussion.
2. Problem recognition: quiet editorial panels using the existing four problem statements.
3. Proof: large case chapters with visible before/after evidence and restrained technical annotations.
4. Method: precise system diagram using the existing process copy.
5. Discussion: a low-pressure final invitation, with case exploration remaining the secondary path.

Technical features should reveal meaning: image comparison, case-layer exploration, reactive diagram lines, progressive type reveals, and spatial transitions. Decorative motion without informational value is excluded. Reduced-motion, touch, keyboard, contrast, and performance fallbacks remain mandatory.

## Recommended direction

Dark Editorial Interface: near-black primary field, warm-white typography, electric pink navigation/proof signal, restrained steel-grey details, cinematic image treatments, oversized type, and an exact modular grid. This combines the references’ cleanliness with Povkh.Dev’s commercial logic.

## Self-review

- Every observation refers to a visible property of the supplied references.
- Preference is separated from functional requirements.
- The main risk includes a concrete correction.
- No new public claim, copy, metric, or case fact is introduced.
